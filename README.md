## About

**JumpSHREK** is a 2D platformer where you play as Shrek, scaling a mountain layer by layer to escape the Dragon and reach Princess Fiona at the top.

Your choices matter. Along the way, you'll encounter iconic characters from the Shrek universe -- each conversation shapes your fate. Make the right calls, and Fiona might just fall for you. Make the wrong ones, and... well, someone else will appreciate your evil.

**Multiple endings. One Ogre. NO REGRETS.**

---

## Features

- Layer-by-layer mountain climbing -- ascend through escalating platformer challenges
- Branching dialogue system -- speak with Puss in Boots, Donkey, and Rumpelstiltskin
- Multiple endings -- your choices determine whether love or chaos wins
- Dragon on your tail -- escape or face the consequences
